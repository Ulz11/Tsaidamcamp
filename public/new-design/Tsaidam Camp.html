The camp is open from June through September. July and August offer the warmest weather and the most vibrant steppe landscape, while June and September bring cooler temperatures and fewer visitors — ideal for a more private retreat.
The camp is located in Bayan soum, Arkhangai Province — approximately 6 hours by vehicle from Ulaanbaatar. We offer airport transfer services upon request. The nearest town is Tsetserleg, about 90 km away.
Tsaidam is intentionally off the grid — part of the experience. There is no Wi-Fi, and mobile signal is minimal. Emergency satellite communication is available at the camp office. We encourage guests to fully disconnect.
All stays include three daily meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) prepared by our on-site chef, one guided activity of your choice, and access to all shared camp facilities. Premium activities such as eagle hunting or multi-day treks are priced separately.
Layers are key — mornings and evenings can be cold even in summer. We recommend sturdy walking shoes, sunscreen, a warm jacket, and a camera. Bedding, towels, and basic toiletries are all provided in your ger or cabin.
